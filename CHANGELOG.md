# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-09-12 — proposed identity system

Rebuilt the initial identity sketch into a corporate identity proposal with an independent graphic system, a complete source package, reusable channel applications, and a governed handoff.

- Set **Catalyst of analysis.** as the owner-directed primary brand idea: counter paralysis of analysis by turning thoughtful analysis into forward motion. The visual direction **Open frame** remains a proposal for review.
- Preserved the original homepage language, including **Better input. Clearer decisions.** and **A quieter way to decide**, as the warm verbal foundation alongside the core idea.
- Established Decision Ink, Paper, Mist, Field Green, Signal Orange, and White as a restrained product palette. Source Sans 3 now carries both display and reading; IBM Plex Mono carries utility metadata.
- Added strategy, concept rationale, messaging, logo standards, color accessibility, typography, imagery, motion, channel guides, brand architecture, product-claim boundaries, contribution rules, and release/adoption records.
- Added a reproducible 16-page landscape visual identity manual, rendered and visually inspected page by page.
- Kept the orange point as the narrow aSUKIra lineage cue. No acquisition claim or Suki dependency is introduced.
- Distinguished identity completeness, user approval, repository publication, website deployment, and live product acceptance.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-12 — superseded sketch

Initial Di mark, brief guidelines, palette/type tokens, and a simple public identity page. Superseded by the complete 0.2.0 system. It is not an approved brand release and must not remain a competing source of standards.

01 / The ideaFrom thought to direction

Better input.
Clearer decisions.

The opposite of paralysis isn’t haste.
It’s a useful way forward.

DecisionInvitation helps a decision owner frame a question, invite people privately, and understand the reasoning behind each response—without a meeting, a group thread, or the loudest voice winning.

“Catalyst of analysis” is the central brand idea. “Ask separately. Decide clearly.” is the product promise. One says why we exist. The other makes the experience understandable.

01

Make room for thinking.

Invite a person’s perspective before the group has a chance to shape it.

02

Make the reasoning useful.

Surface what matters: positions, assumptions, context, and unresolved questions.

03

Keep the responsibility.

Bring perspectives together. The decision remains with the person making it.

Independent by design. A quiet family connection. DecisionInvitation has its own voice, palette, and visual rhythm. Suki Orange is a small signature of its relationship to aSUKIra—not a second identity competing for attention.

Give it room.

Keep at least one dot diameter of clear space around the visible artwork. The breathing room is part of the signature.

Small doesn’t mean approximate.

Use the optically simplified micro mark at 16–32 pixels. It closes the aperture so the silhouette remains readable. Never shrink the full lockup into a favicon.

Keep the signature intact.

  • Use the supplied masters.
  • Keep the proportions and dot position.
  • Choose a high-contrast background.
  • No gradients, effects, outlines, or extra dots.
  • Use one color when color isn’t reliable.

03 / ColorQuiet ground. Precise accent.

Room to think.
A point of energy.

Deep Ink and Paper do most of the work. Mist creates a softer second surface. Field adds a restrained supporting tone. Orange is a signature, never the whole room.

Click a swatch to copy its value. Proportions shown are a compositional example, not a fixed formula.

Contrast is a design rule. Ink on Paper: 13.31:1. Field on Paper: 4.57:1. Orange on Paper: 3.93:1—use it for the mark and non-text accents, not small text. Primary buttons use Paper on Ink.

06 / VoiceConsidered. Direct. Inviting.

There is a decision
waiting for a
better question.

We sound like someone who respects your judgment and knows how to help. No manufactured urgency. No claims that software can make responsibility disappear.

Invite, don’t instruct

“Submit your required input.”

“We’d value your perspective.”

Be precise about privacy

“Your answer is anonymous.”

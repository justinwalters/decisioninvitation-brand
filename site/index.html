WHY IT EXISTS

Better input.
Clearer decisions.

The opposite of paralysis isn’t haste. It’s a useful way forward.

DecisionInvitation helps a decision owner frame a question, invite people privately, and understand the reasoning behind each response—without a meeting, a group thread, or the loudest voice winning.

ASK

Make room for thinking.

Invite a perspective before the group has a chance to shape it.

UNDERSTAND

Make reasoning useful.

Surface positions, assumptions, context, and unresolved questions.

DECIDE

Keep the responsibility.

Bring perspectives together. The decision stays with the person making it.

NEUROINCLUSIVE BY DESIGN

Different minds need different conditions for contributing.

Designed for minds that do their best thinking with context, time, and a clear next step.

DecisionInvitation’s neuroinclusive promise is structural, not clinical: one bounded question, independent response time, visible conditions, resumable progress, and an accountable next step. Those choices are designed to reduce avoidable cognitive and social load. They do not diagnose, treat, or guarantee a better outcome.

CONTEXT

Orient before asking.

Show the question, purpose, audience, visibility, effort, and deadline before someone begins.

TIME

Let thought be resumable.

Support independent, asynchronous contribution with clear state, save-and-return, and factual reminders.

NEXT STEP

Make conditions useful.

Treat “yes, if,” “not yet,” and “I need this fact” as decision data—not failure to choose.

AGENCY

Keep the call human.

The system clarifies perspectives. The named decision owner remains responsible for the consequence.

THE MULTIPATH D

Many inputs.
One point.

The post is absent. The D is unmistakable.

Five independent inputs begin on one invisible axis and converge at a single decision point. Their aligned openings let the eye complete the missing spine. The lowercase i makes the signature specific; its orange dot is a quiet provenance cue for those who know where to look.

Protect the absence.

Never draw the spine. Keep all five inputs aligned, visibly separate, and aimed at the same point. Preserve 32 source units clear on every side.

Orange is provenance.

It belongs only on the lowercase i dot. Never exceed two percent of a composition or use orange as interface hierarchy.

Exact at every scale.

Use the micro master from 16–32 px. Use the full mark at 24 px and above.

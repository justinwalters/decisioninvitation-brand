# Website guidelines

The root product homepage and `/brand` have different jobs. The homepage explains the product and preserves real invitation routes. `/brand` publishes the corporate identity, approved assets, usage rules, and release status. A successful brand-page build does not prove the homepage, invitation flow, DNS, TLS, or deployment.

Use the Decision Instrument grammar: ruled analytical lanes, one commitment threshold, split planes, and precise Sora typography. Keep controls rectangular and explicit. Avoid generic rounded-card dashboards and decorative motion.

Every page must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, reduced motion, semantic headings, useful alternative text, 200 percent zoom, and AA contrast. Links must remain identifiable without color alone. Product claims, privacy language, permissions, and participant visibility must match implemented behavior.

The aSUKIra relationship belongs in a quiet footer or provenance note. It must not become a co-branded masthead.

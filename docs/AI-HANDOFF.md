# AI handoff

Use these rules when generating DecisionInvitation material.

## Non-negotiable identity rules

1. Treat DecisionInvitation as an independent product, not an aSUKIra theme.
2. Use Sora, Register, Cobalt, Periwinkle, Optic, and White.
3. Use Suki Orange only as the tiny lowercase-i dot in the color signature, capped at two percent of a composition.
4. Build layouts from distinct analytical inputs, implied alignment, and one convergence point.
5. Keep corners square, alignment exact, and language plain.
6. Do not invent product capabilities, approvals, certifications, customers, delivery evidence, or acquisition details.
7. Preserve: “Catalyst of analysis.”, “Better input. Clearer decisions.”, “Ask separately. Decide clearly.”, “A quieter way to decide.”, “There is a decision waiting for a better question.”, and “Invite the perspectives. Keep the responsibility.”

## Reject on sight

Reject orange-led palettes, parent-brand fonts, cream/navy editorial pages, typed `Di` substitutes, soft SaaS gradients, rounded card clouds, checkmark symbols, speech bubbles, and decorative data visualizations. Preserve the bespoke Multipath D: five open input terminals aligned on an implied spine, two heavy outer paths, three thin internal paths, and one shared convergence point. Generated material must remain understandable when the logo and orange dot are removed.

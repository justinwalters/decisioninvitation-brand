# Identity handoff

**Version:** 0.2.0 · **Status:** proposal awaiting user review · **Prepared:** 2026-09-12

DecisionInvitation is the **catalyst of analysis**: it helps people move a decision forward when analysis has become circular. A clear question, independent perspectives, and visible conditions give the owner something to act on. Its identity is composed, open, and exact. It must feel established in its own right. A single orange point recalls aSUKIra; the geometry, color balance, vocabulary, and pace belong to DecisionInvitation.

## Read in this order

1. `../brand-system.json` — version, source map, status, identity constants, validation entry points.
2. `brand-strategy.md` — audience, value, creative territories, and proposed direction.
3. `brand-guidelines.md` — the working system and rules that hold across media.

Use `art-direction.md`, `motion-and-interaction.md`, and `brand-architecture.md` when the assignment involves those topics. Use a brief from `../templates/briefs/`.

## Working facts

- Product spelling: **DecisionInvitation**. Speech may naturally separate the two words; designed text and prose preserve the joined name.
- Category descriptor: **A private space for considered decisions.** This describes the proposition; it is not a security certification.
- Primary brand idea, explicitly directed by the user: **Catalyst of analysis.**
- Core tension: **paralysis of analysis**. The product should turn thoughtful analysis into forward motion without rushing judgment.
- Supporting product line retained from the product source: **Ask separately. Decide clearly.**
- The user values the existing homepage language. Preserve its warm verbal foundation: **Better input. Clearer decisions.**; **A quieter way to decide**; **Invite the perspectives. Keep the responsibility.** The core idea adds strategic meaning; it does not require replacing the homepage headline.
- Proposed direction: **Open frame** — a held space with a deliberate opening, a separately legible `i`, and a small orange point.
- Core workflow: frame the question → invite the relevant perspectives → review the reasoning → record the owner's decision. Only show a stage as available when its implementation is verified.
- Current delivery limits are listed in `claims-and-privacy.md`. Brand design does not alter them.

## Inheritance boundary

DecisionInvitation shares the parent system's respect for human authority, legibility, and restrained orange. It does not inherit Suki's broad-brush mark, Lexend display voice, navy palette, Tomorrow Killer stance, product metaphors, or conversion policy. Use the exact aSUKIra asset if a corporate endorsement is actually required; do not approximate it.

## Source priority

User-approved decisions outrank accepted system decisions; then come canonical tokens and source masters, then guidelines and reusable templates. A proposal is a proposal at every layer. Rendered exports, public pages, screenshots, and archived sketches are projections.

For current product positioning and readiness, `../../suki-decisioninvitation/PRODUCT.md` in the sibling checkout takes precedence over older design-plan descriptions. The older mediated-decision design includes useful privacy principles but conflicts with newer product independence and account implementation. Do not resolve backend authority or security disagreements through marketing copy. Record them for the product owner.

## Deliverable receipt

Every handoff states: consumed version; files changed; source assets; tests and visual checks actually completed; remaining exceptions; approval status; publishing/deployment result. A checked source package is not a live website receipt. A remote repository URL is not proof that the remote was created. Never describe a screenshot as an editable master.

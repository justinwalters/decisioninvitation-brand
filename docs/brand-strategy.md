# Brand strategy

**Status:** version 0.2.0. The user has directed the primary brand idea **Catalyst of analysis.**, in direct opposition to paralysis of analysis, and an independent product with a subtle family connection. The user also values the existing warm homepage language; preserve that verbal foundation. The aesthetic expression and newly proposed copy remain proposals for review.

## The human problem

Analysis can stop producing a clearer decision. The same options return in another meeting, another message, another comparison. Questions expand while the next move stays unclear. This is the paralysis of analysis that DecisionInvitation exists to confront.

Important decisions are also discussed in the wrong shape. A group thread rewards the earliest confident answer. A meeting can make it expensive to disagree. A poll captures preference while losing its conditions. The person responsible still needs to understand what each perspective means for the choice in front of them.

DecisionInvitation makes analysis productive again by creating a considered exchange around one decision. The owner frames the question; each invited person receives relevant context and gives a reasoned position; the owner reviews those perspectives and owns the call. The product should convey momentum grounded in consideration. A next step may be a decision, a condition to resolve, or a precisely named missing fact.

## Positioning

For people responsible for a consequential decision, DecisionInvitation is a private decision workspace that turns analysis into a clearer next move. It organizes independent perspectives and their reasoning around a clear question, helping the owner see the conditions that matter and take responsibility for the call.

**Purpose:** turn the paralysis of analysis into considered forward motion.

**Long-term ambition:** a decision record that preserves both the call and the reasons people brought to it.

**Value proposition:** turn a question that keeps circulating into independent perspectives, explicit conditions, and a clearer next move.

These statements express design intent. Claims about actual availability follow the verified product boundary, not the ambition.

## Audience and moment

| Audience | Situation | What earns confidence | What the brand must make easy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision owner | A choice crosses teams, suppliers, advisers, or family roles | Clear question, relevant responses, visible ownership | Define the request and understand what happens next |
| Invited contributor | Someone needs their judgment on one part of the situation | Named requester, explicit scope, deadline, audience for the answer | Know why they were invited and provide a considered response |
| Organizational sponsor | Repeated decisions need a recognizable, manageable process | Accurate visibility rules, accountability, support and operational evidence | Evaluate the product on a concrete workflow |
| Implementation or channel partner | Adapts an invitation to a client context | Stable identity rules and precise product boundaries | Apply an identity without changing the meaning of privacy or authority |

The identity works equally for a supplier reviewing a date, an adviser weighing an option, or a colleague flagging a constraint. Do not equate seriousness with executive seniority, enterprise size, or a particular profession.

## Three creative territories

| Territory | Central thought | Visual expression | Strength | Tension |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open frame** — recommended | Structure creates a way forward | Held rectangular fields, an opening at the edge, calm green and ink, clear Di construction | Expresses a bounded question with a way through; extends naturally into print, product, and motion | Must make the next move visible so openness does not feel indefinite |
| **Considered exchange** | A perspective can move the question | Alternating text columns, offset margins, a shared baseline, quieter punctuation | Shows how a new condition changes the decision; strong editorial and report language | Could drift toward consulting or a general collaboration tool |
| **Decision correspondence** | Turn deliberation into a considered reply | Formal letter proportions, addresses and datelines, a restrained seal, tactile paper | Distinctive, personal, credible in invitations and stationery | Risks institutional nostalgia and placing attention on the message rather than the decision |

Open frame is recommended because it has the most useful range: a recognizable small identifier; an inviting public story; exact, low-noise product surfaces; and enough graphic structure for an extensive corporate package. Correspondence informs the tone of invitations. Considered exchange informs editorial grids. Their marks and visual systems are not mixed into the chosen route.

## The proposed creative idea

**Catalyst of analysis.**

The user's chosen idea names the product's active role: analysis should create movement. The useful response is not automatically a faster yes or a forced consensus. It may expose an assumption, separate two questions, make a trade-off explicit, or identify the one fact the owner needs next. Each of those moves the decision forward.

“Catalyst” is metaphorical brand language. Do not turn it into a scientific performance claim or a promise that software can decide correctly on someone's behalf. Pair the line with the mechanism: frame the question, ask separately, make conditions explicit, decide with ownership. The proposed visual system gives the argument a held space and a clear opening toward the next move.

The strategic idea does not replace every customer-facing headline. **Better input. Clearer decisions.** remains the existing homepage lead, supported by **A quieter way to decide** and **Ask separately. Decide clearly.** The warmer language makes the mechanism inviting; the catalyst idea explains why the product exists. Preserve the full user-valued foundation and its context in [Messaging and voice](messaging-and-voice.md).

The identity carries four qualities:

1. **Purposeful.** Every question and perspective should help clarify the next move.
2. **Exact.** Names, dates, attribution, and conditions are explicit.
3. **Open.** The work welcomes a useful perspective, including disagreement.
4. **Accountable.** Contribution and decision ownership remain distinct.

## Recognition and family resemblance

Recognition should accumulate through a precise `Di`, green-black ink, soft mineral fields, large Source Sans 3 sentences, and one open edge. The orange point is the small inherited detail someone may notice on a second look. It is not the whole identity.

The user described the desired feeling as a mature product subtly rebranded after acquisition. Use that as an art-direction test. It does not establish an acquisition history, legal entity, ownership date, or corporate transaction.

## Strategic checks

A successful execution answers these questions at a glance: What decision is stuck? What could move it forward? What is being asked of me? Who owns the call?

During review, test recall of the product name and mark separately from recall of the orange dot. Ask whether the artifact feels useful to a first-time invitee and credible to an organizational buyer. Check that the proposed headline is understood without a long explanation. These are proposed research questions; this package does not claim completed audience testing.

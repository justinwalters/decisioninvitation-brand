# Brand Strategy

## Category

DecisionInvitation is a decision instrument: a structured way to frame a consequential question, collect perspectives independently, and clarify the next move.

## Strategic idea

**Catalyst of analysis.** The enemy is not analysis; it is analysis without a threshold, owner, or forward condition.

## Position

For people accountable for consequential choices, DecisionInvitation turns distributed judgment into a legible next move while keeping responsibility with the decision owner.

## Audience jobs

- Decision owner: reveal what would change or settle the call.
- Contributor: understand why their view matters, who can see it, and what happens next.
- Organizational reader: see a credible workflow, evidence boundary, and accountable owner.

## Personality

Exact, calm, invitational, decisive. Never coercive, performatively clever, or falsely certain.

## Promise

**Ask separately. Decide clearly.** “Clear” may mean decide, establish a condition, or identify the missing fact. It never means forcing consensus.

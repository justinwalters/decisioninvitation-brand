# Art direction

The visual world is a decision instrument: perspectives enter as separate lanes, cross a fixed commitment threshold, and resolve into one accountable outcome.

## Composition grammar

- Begin with two or three distinct horizontal lanes.
- Use one vertical gate as the moment responsibility changes.
- Place the orange lineage point only beyond that gate.
- Prefer split planes, hard alignment, square corners, and visible rules.
- Use a 58/42 split when a page needs a characteristic asymmetry.
- Let evidence and language occupy real space; decoration stays secondary.

## Avoid

Do not use gradients, soft blobs, glass effects, floating card stacks, speech-bubble marks, checkmarks, orange feature fields, editorial chapter numbers, or the parent identity's cream-and-navy atmosphere. Photography should show deliberation, evidence, or the consequence of a decision—not generic meetings or posed collaboration.

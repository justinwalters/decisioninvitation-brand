# Typography

Sora is the sole brand family. Its geometric construction makes the system feel designed for structured judgment without borrowing aSUKIra's editorial voice.

| Role | Weight | Guidance |
|---|---:|---|
| Display | 400 | Short, sentence-case propositions; tight but never compressed |
| Heading | 600 | Section names, decisions, and clear actions |
| Body | 400 | Plain-language explanation and participant guidance |
| Data label | 600 | Dates, identifiers, stages, and compact metadata |

Use the included Sora Regular and SemiBold files. Do not substitute the parent-brand fonts. Utility information remains in Sora; distinction comes from size, weight, spacing, and rules rather than a separate monospace voice.

Keep body copy between roughly 55 and 75 characters per line. Use sentence case. Avoid all-caps paragraphs, artificial italics, ultra-light display weights, and tightly tracked small text. Interface text must remain legible at 200 percent zoom and in narrow layouts.

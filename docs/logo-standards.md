# Logo Standards

## Commitment Gate

Three independent lanes move toward a fixed vertical threshold. A small point sits beyond the gate, signaling both forward movement and quiet lineage. The mark is intentionally not an initial monogram.

## Approved files

Use outlined masters from `production/01-logo/svg/`. PNG exports have transparency; PDF exports support placement workflows. Use the micro mark below 24 px.

## Scale and space

- Master mark minimum: 24 px digital.
- Micro mark minimum: 16 px digital.
- Lockup minimum: 144 px wide.
- Clear space: 1.5 times the gate width on every side.
- Orange point: never smaller than 2 device pixels.

## Colorways

Primary uses Register with the orange point. Reverse uses White with the orange point. Ink and White variants are one-color accessibility/production alternatives.

## Never

Do not redraw, rotate, outline, soften, animate as a spinner, use the mark as a D or i, add a container, move the point, recolor the point for status, or combine it with the aSUKIra mark.

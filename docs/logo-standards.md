# Logo Standards

## Multipath D

The outer silhouette must read as a capital D before any inner detail is noticed. Its flat spine, strongly defined upper and lower bowl, and open counter carry recognition. Three thinner Periwinkle paths inside the counter preserve the idea of independent perspectives becoming direction. A distinct lowercase i completes the signature; its orange dot is the sole aSUKIra lineage cue.

## Approved files

Use outlined masters from `production/01-logo/svg/`. PNG exports have transparency; PDF exports support placement workflows. Use the micro mark below 24 px.

## Scale and space

- Master mark minimum: 24 px digital.
- Micro mark minimum: 16 px digital.
- Lockup minimum: 144 px wide.
- Clear space: 32 source units on every side.
- Orange point: never smaller than 2 device pixels.

## Colorways

Primary uses Register for the D and i, Periwinkle for the internal paths, and Orange for the i dot. Reverse uses White, Periwinkle, and Orange. Ink and White variants are one-color accessibility/production alternatives; the path hierarchy remains visible through stroke weight.

## Never

Do not redraw, rotate, outline, soften, animate as a spinner, close the counter, thicken the internal paths, merge the i into the D, add a container, move the dot, recolor the dot for status, or combine it with the aSUKIra mark. Do not use typed “Di” as a substitute for the artwork.

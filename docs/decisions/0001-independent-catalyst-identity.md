# 0001 - Independent identity, catalyst strategy

Status: superseded by ADR 0002. The owner-directed verbal strategy remains active; the visual recommendation below is historical.

## Context

DecisionInvitation requires the depth and discipline of the aSUKIra corporate identity program while retaining an unmistakably independent product identity. The design brief calls for the coherence of an established product with a subtle family connection: recognizable on its own, with lineage expressed through a restrained orange detail rather than the Suki mark. This is a design intention, not a statement about acquisition history.

The owner-directed strategic idea is **Catalyst of analysis.** The brand gives thoughtful analysis a way forward and addresses paralysis of analysis without encouraging rushed judgment. **Ask separately. Decide clearly.** remains the supporting product promise.

## Decision and recommendation

Directed: use `Catalyst of analysis.` as the primary brand idea; give thoughtful analysis a way to move forward, without rushing judgment. Keep the product standalone and the family reference subtle. Do not assert a real acquisition.

Recommended: the **Open frame** visual direction, a bespoke open-D/separate-i monogram, a restrained orange point, Ink/Paper/Mist/Field palette, Source Sans 3, and IBM Plex Mono. This differs from the parent's dominant expression while sharing one deliberate color trace and disciplined asset governance.

Considered alternatives are documented in [Brand strategy](../brand-strategy.md): Considered exchange and Decision correspondence. They remain strategic explorations, not alternate production logos. Open frame is recommended because it connects private contribution and a visible way forward without implying a public poll or a legal notice.

## Consequences

The core idea leads the brand reference and manual. The established homepage language, including “Better input. Clearer decisions.” and “A quieter way to decide.”, remains the preferred public-facing foundation. The strategic idea does not mandate replacing the homepage hero; supporting product mechanics explain it. Orange is not a primary action system. The Di is a proposed identity: neither visual approval nor trademark registration is implied. The visual system is generated, measurable, and reviewable, but owner approval remains a separate gate. Product capability and hosting evidence are unchanged by this decision.

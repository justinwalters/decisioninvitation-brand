# ADR 0002: Decision Instrument identity

Status: superseded by ADR 0003

## Decision

DecisionInvitation will use an independent visual system called **Decision Instrument**. Its signature is the **Commitment Gate**: separate lanes approach a hard threshold and resolve into forward motion. The mark is abstract and must not be described as a monogram.

The primary system uses Register, Cobalt, Periwinkle and Optic. Sora is the only brand typeface. Layouts use split planes, ruled lanes and decisive alignment.

The aSUKIra relationship is expressed only by one small Suki Orange lineage point after the gate. Orange is limited to two percent of a composition and is not used for headings, buttons, fields, backgrounds or decoration.

## Why

The previous proposal repeated the parent brand's cream, dark blue-green, orange-dot monogram, editorial pacing and font roles. That made the relationship louder than the product idea. This system makes the product's purpose—turning analysis into responsible commitment—the dominant visual fact.

## Supersedes

ADR 0001's Open Frame visual direction. The verbal strategy remains: **Catalyst of analysis.** and **Ask separately. Decide clearly.**

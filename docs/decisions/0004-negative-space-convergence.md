# ADR 0004: Negative-space convergence

**Status:** accepted as proposal direction; owner review pending  
**Date:** 2026-09-12

## Decision

The Multipath D will have no literal vertical post. Five independent horizontal inputs begin at a shared alignment and converge at one point. The two heavy outer inputs describe the upper and lower bowl of a capital D; three lighter Periwinkle inputs express distinct analytical perspectives. The empty alignment at the left lets the eye infer the missing spine. A separate lowercase i retains the single Suki Orange provenance dot.

## Rationale

The previous filled D improved letter recognition but made the analytical paths feel contained by a conventional shell. Removing the post turns the entire letter into the product idea: separate inputs become a legible decision form by resolving together. The negative-space spine keeps the D recognizable without drawing a closed monogram or imitating the parent identity.

## Constraints

- All five input terminals align on the same invisible vertical axis and remain visibly disconnected.
- All five paths terminate at the same convergence point.
- The two outer paths remain heavy enough to establish the D before the internal detail is noticed.
- The three internal paths remain lighter and thinner in full-color use.
- The lowercase i remains separate; Orange is reserved for its dot and stays below two percent of a composition.
- The identity remains a proposal until explicit owner approval.

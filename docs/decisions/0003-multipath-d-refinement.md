# ADR 0003: Multipath D refinement

**Status:** accepted as proposal direction; owner review pending
**Date:** 2026-09-12

## Decision

The Decision Instrument identity will use a bespoke **Multipath D** signature. A strongly defined capital D is the dominant silhouette. Three lighter, thinner paths live inside its counter to communicate independent perspectives and multiple routes through analysis. A separate lowercase i completes the mark, with its dot in Suki Orange as the only visual provenance cue.

## Rationale

The preceding Commitment Gate was conceptually distinct but did not read clearly enough as DecisionInvitation. This refinement puts recognition first and metaphor second: the D remains legible at a glance, while the multipath idea rewards closer inspection. The lowercase i adds product specificity without importing the parent identity's composition, typography, or palette.

## Constraints

- The upper and lower bowl of the D must remain visibly defined.
- Internal paths must remain lighter in color and thinner than the D silhouette in full-color use.
- Orange is reserved for the lowercase i dot and remains below two percent of a composition.
- The signature is custom artwork; typed “Di” is not a replacement.
- Public prose continues to use the full name `DecisionInvitation`.
- The identity remains a proposal until explicit owner approval.

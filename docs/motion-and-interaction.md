# Motion and interaction

Motion explains transition from analysis to commitment. It never manufactures urgency.

- Feedback and focus changes: about 120 ms.
- Structural transitions: about 260 ms.
- The five inputs may enter independently from their shared, invisible origin.
- Their terminal point remains fixed while each path resolves into it.
- The orange i dot may appear only after all five paths settle.
- Use direct easing and short travel; avoid springy celebration and ambient drift.

Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` by removing travel and using immediate state changes or a brief opacity change. Never make animation the only indication that a decision was recorded, changed, or failed.

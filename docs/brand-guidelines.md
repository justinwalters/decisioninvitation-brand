# DecisionInvitation corporate identity

**System:** Open frame · **Version:** 0.2.0 · **Status:** proposal for user review

DecisionInvitation is a distinct product with a quiet family connection to aSUKIra. The identity is built to carry a public website, invitation, business card, decision brief, social post, and product screen without changing character.

## Name and signature

Use `DecisionInvitation` as one word with capital D and I. Never shorten the product to DI in public prose. `Di` is permitted as the monogram. Preserve the exact product name in accessible text, metadata, domains, and plain-text channels.

The primary signature combines the monogram and name. The standalone monogram serves avatars, favicons, app identity, and locations where the full name is already clear. It is not a status indicator and does not represent a person's response, a confidence level, or a completed decision.

Keep the orange point in the color signature. One-color marks deliberately preserve the geometry in one ink. The parent brand's brush mark, name styling, or slogan does not accompany the primary signature.

See `logo-standards.md` for exact reproduction and placement rules.

## Color behavior

Paper and Mist are quiet working fields; Decision Ink carries text and most deliberate emphasis. Field Green provides a secondary environment. Signal Orange is scarce: the signature point and occasional large editorial punctuation. It is not a blanket call-to-action or error color.

Favor an Ink button with Paper text for a normal-size primary action. Orange does not provide normal-text contrast against any of the standard light fields. All pairings and their limits are documented in `color-and-accessibility.md`.

## Typography and hierarchy

Source Sans 3 makes the same voice useful at both a large headline and a short participant instruction. Use 400 for expressive display, 600 for compact headings and controls, and 400 for reading. IBM Plex Mono is limited to timestamps, identifiers, captions, sequence labels, and specifications.

A headline usually makes one complete human statement. Explanations fit a readable measure. Metadata supports the narrative, and never becomes a paragraph-long aesthetic. See `typography.md` for sizes and fallback behavior.

## The open frame

An open frame is a partial rectangular boundary with one intentional gap. Its role is to hold a question, perspective, image, or editorial statement. Content remains readable if the frame disappears. The opening suggests an invitation without becoming an arrow, checkmark, speech bubble, or animated system signal.

One frame is usually enough for a composition. Use open space and typography for subsequent sections. Product controls remain conventional and recognizable; the device does not replace form boundaries, validation, or interaction states. See `art-direction.md`.

## Layout

Build from an 8-unit spacing rhythm, with 4-unit adjustments where type or controls need optical alignment. A desktop page can use a 12-column grid; editorial compositions may use a 5/7 or 4/8 split. Long prose stays around 55–75 characters per line. At narrow widths, keep content order and let the frame simplify.

Flat fields, thin rules, and intentional whitespace create depth. Rounded pill containers, gradients, gloss, and decorative dashboards are outside this direction. This is a practical visual constraint, not a statement about the quality of other products.

## Image and motion character

Use real materials and human-scale context: a prepared table, a working document, a deliberate pause before someone answers. Portraits should feel candid and permissioned. Illustrations explain perspectives and decisions with simple planes, openings, and relationships. Customer examples must be real and permitted or clearly marked as illustrative.

Motion reveals a relationship or carries a state change. An opening may become visible once; a field may settle in place. Essential content is immediately available when reduced motion is selected. Never animate the orange point as a pulsing “thinking” light.

## Product and corporate truth

The brand idea is **Catalyst of analysis.** It directly counters the paralysis of analysis: thoughtful consideration should create a clearer next move. It does not guarantee agreement, objectivity, faster decisions, better commercial results, anonymity, encryption, or regulatory compliance. Privacy wording must say who sees what, including the owner's access to attributed responses.

The public identity reference may acknowledge the family connection in an About or footer context. Use a verified relationship statement before publication. Never publish “acquired by” based on the creative brief alone.

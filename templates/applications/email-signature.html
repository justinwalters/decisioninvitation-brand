Preparation notes

  • Use Source Sans 3 in the preview environment. The email table deliberately uses Arial because recipient email software may block web fonts.
  • Delete the optional phone line when it is not useful. Do not add a fictional office address, certification, legal entity, or acquisition statement.
  • Use a PNG at twice its displayed size or larger. Keep the monogram in its paper field and retain alt text when images are blocked.
  • Test normal and dark display modes, reply indentation, Outlook, Gmail, and Apple Mail where relevant. This template does not claim completed client certification.

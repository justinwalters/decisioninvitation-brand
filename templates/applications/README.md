**System:** 0.3.0 identity proposal · **Creative direction:** Catalyst of analysis. · **Supporting promise:** Ask separately. Decide clearly.

These are editable HTML/CSS source masters. They are not screenshots, locked PDFs, finished customer communications, or evidence of a deployed workflow. Open [the application index](index.html) from a local HTTP server serving the repository root. The HTML can also be opened directly when the browser permits local fonts and images.

## Application brief

The audience is a decision owner, an invited contributor, or someone encountering the product through ordinary business correspondence. Each artifact should make the next useful thought or action clear. The identity acts as a catalyst for analysis that leads to a responsible decision; it does not promise speed, agreement, objectivity, or a guaranteed outcome.

The system is calm but active: separate perspectives, a precise threshold, a clearly held question, and an identifiable next step. Sora carries both voice and utility. Register, Cobalt, Periwinkle, and Optic make the independent product recognizable. The orange point after the threshold supplies the subtle provenance cue. There are no additional orange decorations, parent-brand layouts, invented endorsement seals, or acquisition claims.

The current assignment's `Catalyst of analysis.` language takes precedence over earlier campaign-line proposals. Source assets and the overall identity remain proposals until user approval is recorded. These applications must not be relabeled approved by exporting or publishing them.

## Editing and reproduction

1. Duplicate the needed HTML source, retaining `application.css` and the dependency hierarchy. Replace bracketed fields and instructional paragraphs in the source. Browser edits are not automatically saved.
2. Use real, authorized contact details. Remove optional fields instead of inventing them. Do not add security, legal, certification, customer, or acquisition claims that have not been verified.
3. Keep the intended audience and the decision owner explicit. Attributed responses are not anonymous. A summary does not make the decision; a non-response does not mean agreement.
4. Duplicate a document page or slide if the content outgrows its measure. Never solve overflow with smaller body text or by clipping substantive content.
5. Review at actual size with the bundled fonts loaded. Check the longest expected name, email, question, deadline, and source citation. Images need useful alt text; the product name remains readable without the logo.
6. For browser print proofs, use 100% scale, no added margins or browser headers, and background graphics enabled. Named `@page` sizes define the intended artboards, but browser and printer support varies. Confirm actual page dimensions after export.
7. Print output is RGB layout proof material. A qualified print supplier must confirm output profile, conversion, PDF/X requirements, bleed, trim boxes, trapping, duplex orientation, stock, ink coverage, and small-type reproduction. No Pantone match, CMYK recipe, PDF/X conformance, or press approval is implied.
8. Before a social upload, recheck the destination’s current dimensions and overlays. These are house composition masters, not current platform-spec certifications. Export an exact artboard, not a browser page with review chrome or guides.
9. Before email use, substitute a real HTTPS PNG URL, complete the `mailto:` link, delete optional lines, and test with images disabled, dark mode, and relevant clients. The reserved `example.invalid` link is intentional until edited.
